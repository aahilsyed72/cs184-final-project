Black Hole
Renderer

Trace curved light paths around a non-spinning black hole.

A C++ ray tracer integrates photon trajectories with RK4. Captured rays form the shadow. Escaped rays sample an equirectangular star map.

Project: milestone report · Sky asset: NASA Deep Star Maps 2020

Current results

Working shadow and sky lensing, plus a thin accretion disk with striped and temperature-based shading.

9.7× fewer steps

Adaptive stepping: 230 steps per ray
Fixed stepping: 2,224 steps per ray

1.48 s per image

1280 × 720, adaptive steps, 8 threads.
5.2× speedup over one thread on Apple M3.

Source: repository milestone benchmarks in index.html and NOTES.md. Times are medians of three runs. Benchmarks use the Stage 1 scene, without the disk.
